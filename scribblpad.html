Why run AI locally?

Because your computer can do more than connect to the cloud.

cloud AI

Every request leaves the device. Latency, cost and availability depend on the network.

local AI

Inference happens on the device. The loop stays closed — no round trip required.

Privacy
Your files, prompts and workflows can stay on your machine.
Latency
Run inference directly on the device instead of sending every request to a remote server.
Economics
For workloads you run repeatedly, use the compute you've already paid for.
Offline
Your AI doesn't have to disappear when your internet connection does.
Control
Choose your models, hardware and workflows instead of being locked into a single cloud provider.

cloud AI is a service. local AI is infrastructure you own.
