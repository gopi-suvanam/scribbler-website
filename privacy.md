## 1. Introduction

Scribbler and ScribblePad ("we", "us", or "our") are committed to protecting your privacy and giving you control over your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose and protect information when you visit our website, enquire about or purchase our products, download or use our software, or interact with our services.

Our products include AI-capable computers, workstations and related hardware, as well as software for running artificial intelligence models, local AI applications, coding agents and workflows.

We aim to follow a privacy-first approach. In particular, our local-AI software is designed to process data on your own device wherever supported, rather than requiring you to send your prompts, documents or code to our servers.

By using our website or services, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required by applicable law, we will request it separately.

## 3. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with us.

### 3.1 Information You Provide

When you contact us, request a quotation, configure a computer, place an order or request support, we may collect:

- Name and contact details, including email address and telephone number.
- Billing and shipping addresses.
- Product configurations, hardware preferences and budget.
- Order details, invoices and transaction references.
- Messages, enquiries, feedback and support requests.
- Information you choose to provide when participating in demonstrations, testing or product feedback.

Please do not submit confidential documents, passwords or sensitive information through general enquiry forms unless the relevant service explicitly requires and protects that information.

### 3.2 Payment Information

If you purchase a product, payment information may be processed by a third-party payment service provider.

We may receive information such as payment status, transaction references, billing details and refund status. Full payment card details are handled by the payment provider where its payment service is used, rather than stored by us, unless explicitly disclosed otherwise.

Payment providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

### 3.3 Website and Technical Information

When you visit our website, we or our service providers may collect technical information, depending on the website configuration and your privacy choices. This may include:

- IP address and approximate location derived from it.
- Browser type, operating system and device information.
- Pages visited, referring URLs and interaction events.
- Date and time of access.
- Diagnostic, security and server-log information.

We use this information where necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve our website and services.

### 3.4 Software and Device Information

Depending on the software features you use and the permissions you grant, the software may process information necessary to operate on your device, such as:

- Operating system and hardware configuration.
- CPU, GPU and NPU capabilities.
- Available memory and storage.
- Installed or selected AI runtimes and models.
- Local application settings and performance measurements.
- Error reports and diagnostic information, where enabled.

The software's access to this information depends on the relevant feature, operating system permissions and configuration.

## 4. Local AI and Your Data

### 4.1 Local Processing

Scribbler is designed to support local AI processing, including running supported models directly on your device.

Where a model and feature operate entirely locally, prompts, documents, code, inputs and generated outputs are processed on your device and are not transmitted to Scribbler's servers merely because you use that local functionality.

We do not require you to upload your private documents or code to our servers solely to use a supported local-inference feature.

### 4.2 No Automatic Access to Your Files

We do not intentionally collect the contents of your local documents, code, prompts, conversations or model outputs through local-only functionality.

Software features that access files, connect to external services or transmit data may behave differently. Such access depends on the feature you choose, the permissions you grant and the services you configure.

You should review the permissions and settings of integrations, agents and external tools before using them with confidential information.
