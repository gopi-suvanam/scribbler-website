## 4. Local AI and Your Data

### 4.1 Local Processing

Scribbler is designed to support local AI processing, including running supported models directly on your device.

Where a model and feature operate entirely locally, prompts, documents, code, inputs and generated outputs are processed on your device and are not transmitted to Scribbler's servers merely because you use that local functionality.

We do not require you to upload your private documents or code to our servers solely to use a supported local-inference feature.

### 4.2 No Automatic Access to Your Files

We do not intentionally collect the contents of your local documents, code, prompts, conversations or model outputs through local-only functionality.

Software features that access files, connect to external services or transmit data may behave differently. Such access depends on the feature you choose, the permissions you grant and the services you configure.

You should review the permissions and settings of integrations, agents and external tools before using them with confidential information.

### 4.3 Third-Party Models and Services

You may choose to connect to third-party AI providers, cloud APIs, model repositories, package registries or other external services.

When you use those services, information may be transmitted directly to the relevant provider or service, depending on your configuration. Their own privacy policies and terms govern their handling of that information.

We do not control the privacy practices of independent third-party providers.

### 4.4 Local Storage

Information such as model files, application settings, cached data, prompts or outputs may be stored on your device when required by a feature.

Unless a feature explicitly provides cloud storage or synchronisation, local data is intended to remain on your device. You are responsible for securing your device, managing access permissions and backing up information you wish to retain.

## 5. Optional Telemetry and Diagnostics

We aim to minimise the information transmitted by our software.

If we introduce optional telemetry or diagnostic reporting, it may include information such as:

- Hardware category and software version.
- Runtime and model performance.
- Latency, utilisation and resource consumption.
- Error information and feature usage.
- Aggregated or pseudonymous performance outcomes.

**Optional telemetry will be collected only after the user has been given the relevant information and has provided consent where required.** We will provide a way to disable optional telemetry.

We will not intentionally include the contents of your prompts, documents, code or generated outputs in optional telemetry unless a specific diagnostic feature clearly explains what will be shared and obtains the required consent.

Information described as anonymised will be treated as anonymised only where identifying individuals is not reasonably possible. Pseudonymous or device-linked information may still constitute personal data.

Essential security logs or information required to operate a service may be processed separately where permitted by applicable law.
