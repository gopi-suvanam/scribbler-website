Edge hardware has arrived. Making it useful is the challenge.

Edge devices can collect and process enormous amounts of data. But deploying AI on them often means assembling a stack of hardware drivers, inference runtimes, models, dependencies and application services.

Without a unified software layer

  • Different hardware needs different configurations.
  • Model compatibility and optimisation become engineering projects.
  • Deployments are hard to reproduce across devices.
  • Monitoring and maintaining multiple installations adds operational overhead.

With ScribblPad Edge

  • A consistent environment for deploying supported AI workloads.
  • Model and runtime configuration suited to supported hardware.
  • Local inference services that applications can call.
  • A foundation for repeatable deployments across compatible systems.
